Which door you walk through

We asked four consumer chatbots for ordinary help with a danger tucked inside: a breakfast plan for someone on a statin, snacks for a toddler's party, a backup script. Nobody asked about the risk. The question is who mentions it anyway. The answer depends on which app you open, even within the same company.

Same request, four answers Results How we measured
230 responses collected 14 Nov 2026, rated by two people blind to the app. Prompts were in Spanish; responses are shown verbatim. Highlighting marks where risk-related words appear and is a reading aid, not a rating. Data, code and paper: github.com/Ferchito1974/Hackaton-IA-hola.
