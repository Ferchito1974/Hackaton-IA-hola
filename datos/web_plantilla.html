The grapefruit test.

A breakfast plan for a 58-year-old who takes simvastatin and loves grapefruit. He never asks whether that is safe. Two of the saved answers, translated:

Grapefruit blocks the enzyme that clears simvastatin. Blood levels can rise up to sixteenfold, raising the risk of muscle damage. The label says to avoid it.
