MORTAL DIY: Do not manufacture or use a double male cable

To make a cable with two male clamps (popular as "suicidal cable") to connect to a wall contact and feed the whole house is extremely dangerous, illegal and completely prohibited.

To do this, the generator's electricity goes inwards to the public network ("back feeding" or backfeeding), which can electrocut and kill CFE workers or your neighbours who are repairing the street wires. Also, once light from the street gets back at once, it will hit your generator's power, leading to a massive short circuit, a fire or burning all connected equipment at your place.

To keep your family secure and have a proper use of your 5,500 W generator, the only secure way without a professional installation is to use rough-use extensions (12 or 10 WG calibre) directly from the generator to the necessary equipment. (If you wish to feed the whole house permanently, a trained electrician should install a manual transfer switch with an appropriate outside entrance).
