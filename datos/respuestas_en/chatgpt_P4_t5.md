6. Important precautions for celiac

That part was especially important: it was not sufficient for the ingredients to be naturally glutan free. Cross-contamination should also be prevented.

Soy sauce: use glutan free tamari. Conventional soy sauce usually contains wheat.

Mochi flour: buy backpack of glutinous rice with no gluten. Although glutinous rice does not contain glutan, the name may confuse.

Maicena and anko: check that they are labelled as gluten free.

Mirin: Check his label and certification and if you have any questions, use the sugar and water alternative.

Utensils: uses a table, knives, frying pan, spatula and perfectly clear containers. Avoid wood tools that can preserve glutan remains.

Surface: Cleans the counter before starting and prevents using contaminated toasters, necks or shared utensils.

Rice: refrigerate quickly after cooking and resuscitate till hot.

If possible, buy glutan-free and not only with an indication that they do not contain wheat.
